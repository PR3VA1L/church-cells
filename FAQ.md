# 📖 Church Cell App — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

A comprehensive guide for Church Administrators, Cell Leaders, and Members.

---

## 📱 1. Member Logins & Phone Numbers

### Q: How do cell members and visitors log in to the app?
**A:** Members and visitors do not need a password. They simply:
1. Go to the app website.
2. Under **Account Type**, select **"Cell Member / Visitor"**.
3. Type their registered **phone number** (e.g. `0712345678`) and click **Sign In**.
4. They are immediately logged into their personal **Member Portal** to view their growth journey and submit weekly pillar assessments.

---

### Q: What about people who do NOT have a phone number (e.g., children, youth, or elderly members)?
**A:** **The Cell Leader can assign them any custom numeric PIN or ID code in place of a phone number.**

* **Example:** You can assign a member the code `5204`, `1001`, or `8721`.
* When that person logs in, they simply type their assigned number (`5204`) into the Phone Number field.
* This allows children, teenagers without personal phones, or members without smartphones to easily access their portal from a shared family device or tablet.

> [!TIP]
> **Best Practice for Custom Codes:**
> - **Avoid starting with `0`:** The system's phone cleaner trims leading zeroes for international phone formats (e.g., `0872` is saved as `872`). Codes like `5204` or `9431` avoid any confusion.
> - **Make each code unique:** Ensure no two people across the church share the same code, as the system logs into the first person matching that number.

---

### Q: Does the phone number / assigned code have to be 10 digits?
**A:** **No.** There is **no minimum or maximum length requirement**. 
* Standard 10-digit mobile numbers (e.g. `0821234567`) work.
* Short 4-digit or 5-digit PINs (e.g. `5204`, `10245`) work just as easily.

---

### Q: What happens if a member types an unrecognized number?
**A:** The system will show a clear, friendly error message:  
> *"Phone number not found in cell roster. Please check your number or contact your leader."*  
The leader can quickly add or correct the number on the **Cell Roster** tab.

---

### Q: How can a leader add or change a member's number?
**A:** 
1. Log in as **Cell Leader**.
2. Go to the **Cell Roster** tab.
3. Click the small **Edit icon (pencil)** next to their phone number.
4. Type the new number or PIN and click the **Checkmark (✓)** to save.

## 👥 2. Roster Categories & Badges

### Q: What do the 4 roster categories mean?
**A:** The app supports four distinct roles:
* **Member (`M`)**: Regular active cell members who participate in weekly meetings and spiritual assessments.
* **Remote Member (`RM`)**: Members who attend online or virtually; they are also included in weekly assessments.
* **Visitor (`V`)**: Guests attending meetings; tracked for evangelism and follow-up.
* **Child U12 (`C`)**: Children under 12; tracked for family attendance but excluded from spiritual evaluations.

### Q: How does a leader change someone's category?
**A:** On the **Cell Roster** table, simply click the colored category pill (badge) next to their name and select the new category from the dropdown.

## 📊 3. Assessments & Growth Analytics

### Q: How are assessment averages calculated on the Dashboard?
**A:** **Averages are calculated ONLY from members who actually took the assessment.**
* Inactive, absent, or unassessed members **do not** pull down the average.
* **Example:** If a cell has 12 members, but only 3 members took the evaluation and scored an average of `4.5 / 5`, the dashboard accurately displays `4.5 / 5` (instead of unfairly deflating the score by dividing by 12).

---

### Q: Can cell leaders still fill in assessments for members?
**A:** **Yes.** Leaders can evaluate members directly under the **Assessments** tab, OR members can self-evaluate on their own phones via the **Member Portal**. Both methods automatically sync into the cell's dashboard.

---

### Q: What happens if the church updates the assessment questions later?
**A:** The app uses **Dynamic Schema Snapshotting**. Whenever an assessment is saved, it snapshots the exact wording of the questions at that time. If church leadership modifies or rewinds questions in the future, past assessments remain displayed under their historical wording without breaking or losing data.

---

## 🔐 4. Cell Leaders & Admin Operations

### Q: What should a leader do if they forget their password?
**A:** 
1. On the login screen, select **Cell Leader**.
2. Choose your cell group and click **"Forgot Password?"**.
3. Enter the email address registered to your cell. An OTP verification code will be sent to your email to securely reset your password.
4. If no email was registered, click **"Notify Admin"** so church administration can reset it from the Admin Panel.

---

### Q: Can church administrators rename a cell?
**A:** **Yes.** In the **Admin Panel**, administrators can click the **"Rename Cell"** button next to any cell to update its name immediately without losing meetings, attendance records, or member rosters.
